How this is computed

A node's share of its parent is its weight over the sum of its siblings' weights, so every leaf holds a fixed fraction of the company and those fractions sum to 1. Allocated is that fraction times your percentage on the node — it sums to 100% across everyone. Earned is the same figure multiplied by how done the node actually is, so it sums to the company's overall progress and being assigned to a not-started node earns nothing. Change the weights to change what the company thinks is important.
